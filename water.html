Daily Water Intake

--
Liters/day

Your weight in kilograms is used to estimate how much water your body needs daily. The heavier your body, the more water you need to stay hydrated. Height can give context for overall body size, but weight is the main factor in calculating water intake. By entering your weight, the calculator estimates the minimum liters of water you should drink each day to support proper cell function, temperature regulation, digestion, and overall health.
