Waist-to-Height Ratio Calculator

--
Risk Category

The Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR) is a simple measure to assess your risk for cardiovascular and metabolic diseases. A ratio less than 0.5 is considered healthy, while a ratio 0.5 or higher indicates increased risk. By entering your waist and height, this calculator quickly identifies whether your ratio is within a healthy range.
